03Research Experience

Research Intern — Mapping Pigeon Feeding Hotspots, Brussels

Mar – Jun 2026

Université de Namur, Belgium — B-POHOW research project

  • Screened more than 175 candidate locations through bicycle-based fieldwork on a standardised observation protocol, confirming 115 active feeding hotspots and collecting 1,503 photographs and videos (17 GB).
